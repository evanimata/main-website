POSTER: Observing and Obtaining a Light Curve from a Potential Transiting Exoplanet

Derek Kaplan, Evan Imata, Tommaso Frigerio, Xinze Guo, Nadia Laswi, Anders Liu, Jeffrey Martinez

Dates of Research Involvement: September 2021 — May 2022

Exoplanets are planets beyond our solar system, and there are various astronomical techniques used to detect them. Here, we analyze data from the Transiting Exoplanet Survey Satellite (TESS) database to identify potential exoplanet candidates based on physical characteristics (mass, orbital period, distance from star). Then, we collect optical data from our top candidate by operating the Leuschner Observatory.
